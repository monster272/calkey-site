Logging a meal by search means picking from dozens of near-identical entries, then fixing the serving size, for every food, every meal. That is the step that makes people stop. In Calkey you type the meal like a note and it lands in your journal in seconds.

No search, no pickingWrite the meal as you'd text it. Calkey splits it into foods and portions and fills in the figures.
No confirm buttonThe meal logs the moment it's read. If it's wrong, Undo is right there, and any portion changes in two taps.
Your usual meals, instantlyA meal you've logged before comes back from your own history, offline, before you finish typing it.

Questions

What if it gets a portion wrong?

Hold the meal and pick ½, ¾, 1½ or 2×, or open it and type the exact figure. Every change can be undone.

Does it work offline?

You can write meals offline. They are saved at once and filled in when you're back online; meals you've logged before fill in straight away.
