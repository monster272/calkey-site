How big is a cup? It depends on the country

240ml
A US cup. A chai cup in India is about 150 ml

"1 cup" means different amounts around the world. Calkey reads a bare "cup" by the country your phone is from, so 1 cup of chai is 150 ml in Pune and 1 cup of rice is 240 ml in Ohio. And because a cup measures volume, what it weighs depends on the food.

Where the numbers come from

Calories from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy) per 100 g: cooked white rice 130, cooked pasta 158, whole milk 61, plain whole-milk yogurt 61, oats 389. The grams are the cup weights Calkey uses.

Questions

Why does a cup of oats weigh less than a cup of milk?

A cup measures volume. Dry oats hold a lot of air, so a cup weighs about 80 g, while milk fills it at about 244 g.

How many calories in a cup of chai?

About 80 kcal in a 150 ml chai cup with milk and two teaspoons of sugar, and about 40 without sugar.

Is a xícara the same as a US cup?

No. A Brazilian xícara and a Latin American taza are about 200 ml, smaller than a US cup of 240 ml.
