How much is a katori?

150ml
1 katori, the small bowl of an Indian meal

A katori holds about 150 ml. What that weighs depends on the food: dal, sabzi and curd fill it to about 150 g, while cooked rice packs more loosely and weighs about 125 g. These are the sizes Calkey uses when you type "1 katori".

Questions

Is a katori the same as a cup?

No. A katori is about 150 ml, the size of a chai cup, and a US cup is 240 ml. Calkey reads a bare "cup" by the country your phone is from: a chai cup in India, a US cup in the US.

How many katori are in a plate?

Calkey counts a plate of one dish as about 2 katori, so a plate of rice is about 250 g.

What if I don't say how much?

Calkey uses the usual single serving and labels the figure an estimate, so you can change it in two taps.
