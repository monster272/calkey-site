Write your meal in Hinglish. Or Hindi.

“do roti aur ek katori dal”, written as one line

Most of us think about food in the words we cook with, not in a database's English. Translating "ek katori rajma" into a search term, then guessing the serving, is extra work at every meal. Calkey reads the meal as you'd say it.

Any mix of languagesHinglish, Hindi in Devanagari, English, Portuguese, Spanish and Bahasa all work, even within one line.
Thoda, zyada, half plateEveryday amounts count: thoda is about half a serving, zyada about one and a half, a full plate one and a half to two.
Answers in your figuresCalories, protein, carbs and fat for each food, with the reasoning one tap away.

Questions

Is the app itself in Hindi?

Yes, the app can be set to Hindi, and you can type in any of these languages whatever the app's language is.

Can I speak instead of type?

Yes. Tap the mic and say the meal; it's written into the line and logged.
