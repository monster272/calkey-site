    Five steps from a line to a number.

    What happens in the seconds after you press send.

    1. 1

      You write it

      Typed or spoken, in any language and any order. The meal is saved before anything else happens, so it is never lost.

    2. 2

      It reads it

      Each food, its amount and your kitchen's measure: a katori, a concha, a US cup.

    3. 3

      It looks it up

      Regional dishes from a reference table. Packaged and branded food from a live web search.

    4. 4

      It checks the sums

      Calories have to agree with protein, carbs and fat. A weight you state, like 200 g, is multiplied exactly.

    5. 5

      It lands on your page

      Logged at once, with every line explained and a likely range when a portion is a guess.

    Log from wherever you already are.

    A reminder, the lock screen, the home screen, another app. Often the fastest way to log is not to open Calkey at all.

    Before you ask.

    Where can I get it?

    On Google Play, for Android. It is in India for now, and more countries follow soon.

    Is it on iPhone?

    Not yet. Calkey is on Android only for now.

    Which languages can I write in?

    Any language you'd text in: English, Hindi, Hinglish, Spanish, Portuguese, Bahasa, Arabic and more, mixed however you like. The app itself is in English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, Indonesian and German.

    How does it know my country's measures?

    Calkey works out your country from your phone the first time you open it, and keeps that answer. It only decides what plain words like "cup" and "bowl" mean. Words such as katori, concha or centong mean the same thing everywhere, and a size you name, like "200 ml", always wins.

    Do I have to weigh my food?

    No. Say "a cup", "2 slices", "1 katori", whatever you'd normally say. If you do know the weight, type it and Calkey does the exact sum.

    What can my partner see?

    Only today's totals: calories against the goal, protein, carbs and fat, water and the streak. Never the meals or the words you typed. You pair with a one-time code, one person at a time, and either of you can stop sharing whenever you like. Notifications are for goal moments only, at most once a day each.

    What will it cost?

    Calkey is a subscription, yearly or monthly, starting with a 3-day free trial through Google Play.

    Is this medical advice?

    No. Calkey gives estimates to help you track. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor or dietitian for medical guidance.
