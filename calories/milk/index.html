How we know

Derived from IFCT 2017 whole cow's milk (67 kcal per 100 ml): 168 kcal against the 160 we use. Toned milk is about 120.

Questions

Toned or full-fat?

Toned milk has the same protein and less fat: about 120 kcal a glass against 160.

How big is a glass?

Calkey counts a glass as 250 ml unless you name another size.
